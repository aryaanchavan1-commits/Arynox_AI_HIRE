# Privacy

## Data We Collect
- Account information (name, email)
- CV content (when uploaded)
- Interview responses and transcripts
- Assessment answers
- GitHub data (when connected)
- Proctoring signals (face, tab, camera status)

## How Data Is Used
- AI processing for interview evaluation
- Generating candidate reports
- Analytics for hiring teams
- Improving AI accuracy

## Camera & Microphone
- Explicit consent required before interview
- Used for speech-to-text and proctoring signals only
- Not stored permanently
- Not used for emotion recognition or appearance evaluation

## AI Fairness
- Evaluates only job-related technical skills
- Does not evaluate race, religion, gender, caste
- Does not evaluate appearance, accent, or camera quality
- All assessments require human review

## Your Rights
- Access your data
- Correct inaccuracies
- Request deletion
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent

## Data Retention
- Active hiring data retained during process
- Completed data retained per organization policy
- Deletion available on request
